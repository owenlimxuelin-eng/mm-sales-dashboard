Operational Handbook

Quick reference for system rules and emergency protocols.

1. Understanding Pipeline Stages

  • Stage 1: Cold: The initial outreach message was sent. The prospect has not replied yet.
  • Stage 2: Engaged: The prospect replied. AI (Brain 1) is currently chatting with them to build interest and qualify.
  • Stage 3: Ready: The prospect showed strong interest. AI (Brain 1) has provided the group invite link. We are waiting for them to join.
  • Stage 4: Joined VIP: The prospect successfully joined the community. AI (Brain 2) takes over to provide onboarding and support.

2. Human Takeover Protocol (Stage X)

When a lead asks a complex question, click 🤝 Takeover in the CRM. The bot stops instantly, and the lead turns RED. Staff can converse manually. Click Resume AI to return control to the bot.

3. Bulk & Individual Broadcasts

The Broadcast tab allows you to send mass updates.
  • By Stage: Automatically hits everyone in a specific step of the funnel (e.g., pinging everyone in Stage 3 who hasn't joined).
  • Individual: Hand-pick specific users from your CRM using the checkbox list.
  • Media: You can attach a photo or video to your blast (perfect for sending chart images).

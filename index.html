Operational Guide & Manual
Module 1: CSV Lead Data Format
Upload CSV files containing phone_number and name. Phone numbers are automatically cleaned to E.164 international format (e.g. 6585791271).
Module 2: Stage 3 & Community Onboarding
When prospects express intent to join the WhatsApp group, AI flags them as Stage 3: Ready for Group. Send them the link manually, then click ✅ Acknowledge Joined Community to permanently log the timestamp and disable AI automation.
Module 3: Human vs AI Takeover Protocol
Click 🤝 Human Takeover at any time to pause AI. When ready, click 🤖 AI Takeover to allow the AI to catch up on human notes and resume automatically.
